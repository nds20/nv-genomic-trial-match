How the platform works

  1. An administrator invites a verified doctor; there is no public sign-up.
  2. The doctor registers with an NPI and state license, confirms MFA, and waits for admin approval.
  3. Research submissions always enter pending_review.
  4. Only a reviewer or admin can approve research into the live dataset.
  5. The Lenhard engine compares a strict biomarker list with trials stored in Cloudflare D1.

Safety boundary

Use de-identified biomarker symbols only. Do not upload patient records, free-text notes, or protected health information. This prototype is not a clinical decision-support system, does not determine eligibility, and is not certified as HIPAA compliant.
