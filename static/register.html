Registration is invite-only

There is no public sign-up endpoint. An administrator must issue a single-use invite. If you received a registration link, open that exact link; it carries the invite token into the React registration form.

Open the invited registration form

What you will need

  • Your invite token
  • Legal name and email
  • Password of at least 12 characters
  • Individual NPI
  • State license number and two-letter license state
  • An authenticator app for mandatory MFA
